MM02 Mill at Dusk

How the finished model should look on the sill once the sails turn and the four pixels are lit. Proportions follow the kit: 305 mm to the top of the cap and a 355 mm sail span. The controls below behave like the real Home Assistant entities.

5 rpm
120 BPM

Disco Mode chases the four pixels round the mill: each light flashes once per step, a quarter step after the one before, and the order reshuffles every bar. The colour scheme holds for a 16-beat phrase, then changes. Disco Rate runs the chase at half, normal or double time; 2× is only offered while each light stays at three flashes a second or fewer, the usual safe limit. Tap Disco Tap on the beat: one tap lines the flashes up with the music, four or more set the tempo too. It all stays inside the 60% brightness cap. On the real mill this is the next firmware feature, after the bench build.
